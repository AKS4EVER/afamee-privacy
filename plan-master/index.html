1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Plan Master, developed by AFAMEE.

Plan Master is a personal planning, roster, event and reminder application. Its core design is local-first: most planning information is stored and processed on your device rather than being sent to an AFAMEE server. An internet connection is not required for the core planning and locally scheduled reminder functions.

Plan Master does not require you to create an AFAMEE account to use its core features.

2. Information Plan Master handles

Depending on the features you use, Plan Master may handle information that you enter or create, including:

  • shifts, work/off roster patterns and shift profiles;
  • events, appointments, personal activities, tasks and reminders;
  • dates, times, recurrence settings, snooze settings and completion status;
  • notes, preparation and follow-up items;
  • work-hour, overtime, public-holiday and optional pay/earnings information;
  • photos, documents and other attachments you choose to associate with records;
  • app preferences, notification settings and other configuration data.

Core planner content is used to provide the features you request, such as displaying your schedule, calculating summaries, generating reminders, showing widgets, and producing user-requested shares or documents.

AFAMEE does not operate a server that receives your ordinary Plan Master schedule simply because you create or edit it. Optional online services described later in this policy are separate.

3. Local storage

Plan Master stores core application records in local application storage on your device. Where Plan Master creates or owns attachment copies, those files are intended to be kept in application-controlled storage.

Your operating system or device manufacturer may provide device-level backup or restore features. Such platform backups are controlled by the operating system and your device/account settings and are separate from any optional in-app Google Drive backup feature described below.

4. Permissions and device capabilities

Plan Master may request or use device capabilities only where they support a feature you use. Depending on your device and app version, these may include:

  • Notifications to deliver reminders and planning alerts.
  • Exact alarm / alarm scheduling access where supported and required for time-sensitive Alarm Style reminders.
  • Full-screen reminder presentation where Android permits it for high-attention reminder experiences.
  • Vibration and audio controls for reminder presentation.
  • Boot, time and timezone events so scheduled reminders can be reconciled after restart or clock/timezone changes.
  • Photo, camera or file picker access when you choose to attach or capture content.
  • Home Screen Widget capability when you add or use the Plan Master widget.

Availability and permission names differ by Android version and device. Denying an optional permission may prevent the related feature from working but should not prevent use of unrelated core features.

5. Photos, files and attachments

Plan Master may allow you to select or capture photos and choose files such as documents, PDFs, spreadsheets, audio or other supported attachments. Access occurs when you choose the relevant feature.

Plan Master does not intentionally delete the original copy of a file or photo that belongs to your Gallery, Downloads folder, or another external source merely because you remove a Plan Master record. Plan Master may maintain its own copy or reference as required by the version and platform implementation.

Temporary items may be configured to expire. When a Temporary item expires, Plan Master may remove the Plan Master record and any Plan Master-owned copy associated with that Temporary item. The original external source file is not intended to be deleted by this process.

6. Notifications, alarms and text-to-speech

Plan Master uses local notification and alarm mechanisms to deliver reminders that you schedule. Reminder titles or context may appear in system notifications, on a Plan Master Alarm or Call Style screen, or in a Home Screen Widget depending on the options you select and what the operating system permits.

If you use a spoken reminder or Call Style text-to-speech feature, relevant reminder text may be provided to the text-to-speech engine selected on your device. A device or third-party TTS provider may have its own privacy practices, which are outside AFAMEE's control.

Plan Master may retain recent in-app notification history so you can review alerts even after an Android notification has been cleared. Clearing an in-app notification does not necessarily delete the underlying Shift, Event or Reminder record.

7. Sharing, PDFs and printing

Plan Master can create content such as roster/calendar images or PDF documents and may let you share or print them. These actions occur only when you choose them.

When you select Share, Print, Open with, or a similar system action, the content you selected may be transferred to the app, printer service, cloud service or recipient you choose. Once content is sent to another service, that service's privacy practices apply.

8. Optional Google Drive backup and restore

Some versions of Plan Master may offer optional Google Drive backup and restore. If and when this feature is available, it will be user-initiated and will not be required for core planning and reminder functions.

If you choose to use Google Drive backup, Plan Master may transfer the planner data and eligible Plan Master-owned attachments needed to create or restore your backup through Google's services and your Google account. Google may process account, authentication, file and technical information as necessary to provide Google Drive.

Plan Master will request only the Google access needed for the implemented backup feature. Google Drive data is also subject to Google's applicable privacy terms and your Google account settings.

9. Optional advertising

Free versions of Plan Master may display advertising, including through Google Mobile Ads / Google AdMob, when advertising is enabled in that version.

Advertising providers may process information such as device or app identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other information used to deliver, measure, secure or limit advertising. The exact data handled depends on the advertising configuration, your region, consent choices, device settings and the provider's policies.

Where required, Plan Master will provide appropriate consent or privacy choices before using advertising features that require them. Plan Master Pro may remove advertising where that entitlement is available.

10. Optional Plan Master Pro purchases

Some versions may offer a one-time Plan Master Pro purchase through Google Play or another platform store.

Payment processing is handled by the relevant app store. AFAMEE does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Plan Master may receive or verify limited purchase and entitlement information, such as the purchased product and information needed to confirm or restore access.

Plan Master backup data is not used as proof of a Pro purchase; the relevant platform store remains the authority for purchase restoration.

11. Third-party services

Depending on the version and features you use, Plan Master may interact with platform or third-party services including:

  • Google Play and Android system services;
  • Google Drive for optional backup and restore;
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob for optional advertising;
  • your selected text-to-speech engine;
  • apps or services you deliberately choose when sharing, printing or opening files.

Those services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. AFAMEE is responsible for configuring Plan Master and its integrations in a manner intended to comply with applicable platform requirements.

Plan Master's V1 product direction does not include behavioral product analytics. If analytics or another materially different data-processing service is introduced later, this policy and applicable store disclosures will be updated.

12. Data retention and deletion

Ordinary locally stored planner data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, clear/reset app data where such a feature is available, or uninstall the application, subject to any operating-system backup or restore behavior.

Temporary items follow the expiry you configure. Once an item is logically expired, Plan Master is intended to stop presenting it as active and stop delivering future reminders from it. Physical cleanup of app-owned files may occur at the next reliable maintenance opportunity permitted by the operating system.

Information already shared or exported to another app, person, printer, or service is outside Plan Master's local deletion control.

If an optional cloud backup feature is used, retention of those backups is subject to the controls provided by the backup feature, your Google account and Google's applicable policies.

13. Security

AFAMEE designs Plan Master to minimize unnecessary transmission of core planning data. Local application data is kept within the storage mechanisms provided by the operating system, and optional network services should use the secure transport provided by the relevant platform or service.

No software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate device security, such as a device lock and control over access to your Google or app-store account.

14. Children's privacy

Plan Master is a general productivity application and is not designed specifically for children. The core app does not require an AFAMEE account. If a version of Plan Master is offered to an audience for which additional age, consent, advertising or family protections are required, AFAMEE will apply the relevant platform and legal requirements to that version.

If you believe personal information relating to a child has been provided to AFAMEE through a support interaction, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Plan Master will continue to evolve. AFAMEE may update this Privacy Policy when features, integrations, legal requirements or data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective or revision date.
