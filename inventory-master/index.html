Your business data

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Inventory Master, developed by AFAMEE.

Inventory Master is an offline-first inventory, sales and business-management application. Its core design keeps ordinary business data on your Device and processes it locally. An internet connection is not required for core local inventory and sales operation.

Inventory Master does not require you to create an AFAMEE account to use its core features.

AFAMEE does not operate a central Inventory Master business database that automatically receives or stores your ordinary business records simply because you create or edit them in the app. Optional online features described later are separate and apply only when available and used.

2. Business information Inventory Master handles

Depending on the features available in your version and the features you choose to use, Inventory Master may handle information such as:

  • Business Profile information, business name, address, contact details, currency and tax-registration information;
  • Product and Service information, product codes, barcodes, classifications, tags, units, variants, prices, costs, stock quantities and related settings;
  • sales, returns, stock movements, purchase or receipt information and other business-operation records;
  • invoice, receipt, quote, order, payment, discount and tax information where those features are available;
  • customer, supplier or other party information that you choose to enter;
  • Owner or Staff names, Device names, permissions, operational attribution and other identity/provenance information where those features are available;
  • notes, images, attachments, preferences, backup metadata and other configuration or records you deliberately create.

This information is used to provide the business functions you request, such as inventory tracking, stock calculations, sales and return processing, document generation, search, reports, backup, restore, synchronization and other enabled features.

You are responsible for deciding what lawful business, customer, supplier, staff or other information you enter into Inventory Master and for using that information in accordance with obligations that apply to your business.

3. Local storage and data ownership

Inventory Master's core business records are stored in application-controlled local storage on your Device. AFAMEE does not claim ownership over the business content you enter or create in Inventory Master.

Your operating system, Device manufacturer or account provider may separately offer Device-level backup, migration or restore features. Those platform services are controlled by their own settings and policies and are separate from any optional Inventory Master backup feature.

Local-first design does not mean data is impossible to lose. Uninstalling the app, clearing application data, losing or damaging a Device, storage failure, an unsuccessful Device migration, a reset action or other events may remove locally stored data.

4. Sales, invoices and transaction records

Inventory Master may store transaction information required to preserve business history, including products or services sold, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, returns, payment information, transaction references, invoice information, timestamps and operation provenance where those capabilities exist.

Historical documents and transaction records may intentionally preserve snapshots of names, prices, tax treatment, units, customer information or other facts that were valid when the transaction was finalized. Editing current Product, Business Profile or Tax settings does not necessarily rewrite historical transaction meaning.

Generated Invoices, Receipts, reports and other documents may contain business or customer information you selected or that is required by the enabled workflow.

5. Customer, supplier and staff information

Some versions or features may allow you to store customer, supplier, Owner or Staff information. This may include names, business details, contact information, notes, identifiers, roles, permissions, activity attribution or related operational information where you choose to use those features.

Inventory Master uses such information to provide the requested business workflow. AFAMEE does not independently obtain your customer or supplier lists merely because you install the app.

If Staff or shared-Device features are available, the Owner is responsible for deciding who is authorized to use the business dataset and what access or permissions are appropriate.

6. Product images, barcode, QR and camera features

Inventory Master may allow you to select or capture Product images, scan barcodes or QR codes, or choose files through system pickers. Camera or file access is used when you invoke the related feature.

Barcode and QR scanning may process camera frames on the Device to identify supported codes. Inventory Master does not need to upload ordinary camera frames to AFAMEE merely to perform local scanning.

Unsupported or general web QR content must not be treated as trusted Inventory Master business data merely because it was scanned inside the app.

Storage, backup and sharing

7. Backup, export and restore

Inventory Master may allow you to create application backup or export files containing business information. These files are created at your request and may contain substantial business data.

You are responsible for choosing where those files are stored, who receives them, how long they are retained and whether the destination remains accessible and secure.

A backup is not guaranteed merely because a backup feature was enabled. You should verify that important backups completed successfully and remain accessible. If all usable copies are lost, deleted, damaged, overwritten or inaccessible, AFAMEE may have no technical means to recover your business data.

Restore or import features can only work from a valid, supported and accessible source. AFAMEE does not possess a private recovery copy of your business database that can be supplied on request.

8. Secure exports

Some versions may offer encrypted or PIN-protected export or backup options. Those protections are intended to reduce unauthorized access to the exported file but cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for remembering and protecting any PIN, password or credential required to open your protected export. AFAMEE may be unable to decrypt or recover a protected file if the required credential is lost.

Device-local security settings or credentials are not automatically transferred merely because business data is backed up or restored.

9. PDFs, sharing, printing and external apps

Inventory Master may create Invoices, Receipts, reports, labels, images, spreadsheets, PDFs or other files and allow you to share, print, save or open them. These actions occur when you choose the relevant feature.

When you use Share, Print, Open with, Save, a system file picker or a similar action, the selected content may be transferred to another app, person, printer, Device, storage location or service. Those destinations are outside Inventory Master's local control and may have their own privacy and retention practices.

Removing a record or file from Inventory Master does not delete copies that were already exported, printed, forwarded, downloaded, photographed, backed up or stored elsewhere.

10. Optional Google Drive and cloud features

Some versions of Inventory Master may offer optional cloud features, including Google Drive backup, restore or later multi-Device synchronization. These sections apply only if such a feature is available and you choose to connect or use it.

If you use optional cloud backup, eligible business data and app-managed files required for the backup may be transferred to the cloud provider through your authorized account. Inventory Master may also read or validate selected backup files when you ask to list, restore, reconcile or otherwise use them.

If future multi-Device synchronization is enabled, information required to reconcile authorized Devices may be transferred through the selected cloud provider. Inventory Master remains designed so ordinary local operation does not require continuous cloud connectivity.

When a cloud feature is connected, Inventory Master may process technical account and authorization information required to access the selected provider, such as provider account identity, authorization tokens, folder or file identifiers and synchronization metadata. AFAMEE does not require or receive your Google account password. Provider credentials are not ordinary Inventory Master business records and are handled according to the security design of the enabled feature.

AFAMEE does not operate Google Drive or another third-party cloud account. Availability, retention, access, provider-side deletion or alteration, quotas and account recovery are controlled by the selected provider and your account. AFAMEE cannot guarantee recovery of cloud files that are no longer available to you.

11. Optional online Invoice publication

Some versions may allow the Owner to publish selected Invoice documents to a supported external provider and create a link or QR code for external viewing.

This feature is optional. If enabled, the selected Invoice may become accessible to another person who possesses the active link or QR reference, depending on the sharing configuration of the external provider.

You are responsible for deciding which documents are published and for understanding that a link or QR code can be copied, forwarded, photographed or retained by others. Revoking a provider link may not remove copies that were already downloaded or shared elsewhere.

The authoritative local Sale/Invoice record remains separate from the external publication. External provider availability does not determine whether the local transaction exists.

Device and optional services

12. Permissions and device capabilities

Inventory Master may request or use Device capabilities only where they support a feature you use. Depending on your Device, platform and app version, these may include:

  • Camera for barcode/QR scanning or Product image capture.
  • Photos, media or file picker access when you choose images, attachments, backups or import/export destinations.
  • Notifications for enabled stock, reminder, expiry, backup or other business alerts.
  • Internet/network access for optional store, advertising, cloud, publication, licensing, support or other online features.
  • Printing/share capabilities when you deliberately send content to another app, printer or destination.

Permission names and behavior vary by Android or other platform versions. Denying an optional permission may prevent the related feature from working but should not prevent unrelated local features where technically possible.

13. Optional purchases and licensing

Some editions or versions may offer Trial, Pro, paid upgrade or other licensing through Google Play, Apple or another supported store. The platform store processes the purchase under its own terms and privacy practices.

Inventory Master may receive or verify limited purchase and entitlement information needed to determine whether a Trial, Pro or paid capability is available, restore an entitlement or prevent invalid access.

Business backup data is not a substitute for the platform's purchase or entitlement authority.

14. Optional advertising, diagnostics and analytics

Some editions or versions of Inventory Master may display advertising or use diagnostics, crash reporting or analytics services when those services are enabled in that version. If no such service is enabled in the version you use, this section does not mean that advertising or analytics data is being collected.

When advertising is enabled, an advertising provider may process information such as app or Device identifiers, IP address, approximate network-derived location, ad interactions, diagnostics and consent choices, depending on configuration, platform rules and applicable law.

Where diagnostics or analytics are enabled, the provider may process technical information such as app version, Device/OS information, crash details, performance information or feature-usage events configured for that service. AFAMEE does not intentionally configure diagnostics or analytics services to transmit ordinary business-record contents merely for diagnostics or usage measurement. If a future feature materially requires different processing, that processing will be separately disclosed as applicable.

Relevant consent, privacy controls and store disclosures will be applied where required for the released version.

15. Third-party services

Depending on the version and features you use, Inventory Master may interact with platform or third-party services such as:

  • Google Play, Apple or other platform/store services;
  • Google Drive or another supported cloud provider for optional backup, restore, publication or synchronization;
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob or another advertising provider if advertising is enabled in that edition;
  • diagnostics or analytics providers if enabled in that version;
  • apps, printers, file providers or services you deliberately select when sharing, printing, saving or opening files.

Those services are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Provider authentication or permission does not by itself become Inventory Master Business, Owner, Staff or transaction identity.

Support communications. If you contact AFAMEE by email or another support channel, AFAMEE may receive your email address, the information you include in your message, and any files or screenshots you choose to provide. This information is used to respond to your request, investigate issues and provide support. Please avoid sending unnecessary confidential business information. Support communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the matter, maintain appropriate support records, prevent abuse or meet applicable legal obligations.

Responsibility, retention and rights

16. Retention, deletion and recovery limits

Ordinary locally stored business data remains on your Device until it is edited or deleted through the app, removed by an authorized reset or cleanup process, cleared by the operating system or user, or lost through uninstall, Device failure, storage failure or another event.

Some business and historical records may intentionally be retained because they form part of transaction, audit, tax, document or other business history. Exact deletion or archive behavior depends on the feature and version.

Information already exported, shared, printed, published, backed up or synchronized outside the local app may remain in those external destinations even after a local record is deleted.

If optional cloud features are used, retention of cloud files is subject to Inventory Master's available controls, your provider account and the provider's policies and capabilities.

AFAMEE cannot reconstruct business data that no longer exists in any usable copy available to you. Support may help explain available restore steps, but AFAMEE cannot remotely retrieve a database that was never stored on an AFAMEE business-data server.

Privacy requests. Where applicable law provides rights concerning personal information processed by AFAMEE, you may contact us using the address below. AFAMEE can only act on information that it actually controls. Because ordinary Inventory Master business data is stored locally or in services/accounts controlled by you, AFAMEE generally cannot remotely access, correct or delete that local business database on your behalf.

17. Security and user responsibilities

AFAMEE designs Inventory Master to minimize unnecessary transmission of core business data and to use platform security mechanisms and governed app controls appropriate to the enabled features.

No software, Device, backup, encryption method or online provider can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate Device security, protecting PINs/passwords/credentials, controlling who can access your business Devices and accounts, and maintaining suitable backups.

You are also responsible for deciding when to export, share, print, publish, back up or otherwise make business information available outside Inventory Master. Once information is given to another person, app, Device, storage provider or external service, AFAMEE may no longer be able to control, retrieve, revoke or delete every copy.

If an exposure results from a Device, account, file, link, credential or external service under your control, AFAMEE may have no technical ability to retrieve or erase copies already obtained by others. This does not limit obligations that may apply if a disclosure is caused by Inventory Master or AFAMEE itself.

18. Children's privacy

Inventory Master is a business/productivity application and is not designed specifically for children. The core app does not require an AFAMEE account.

If a version is offered to an audience for which additional age, consent, advertising or family protections are required, AFAMEE will apply the relevant platform and legal requirements to that version.

If you believe personal information relating to a child has been provided to AFAMEE through a support interaction, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

19. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Inventory Master will continue to evolve. AFAMEE may update this Privacy Policy when features, integrations, legal requirements or data practices materially change. The current version will be published at this URL with an updated effective or revision date.

The optional-feature wording in this policy is intended to cover known planned capabilities only when they are actually available or used. It does not permit materially different undisclosed processing. If Inventory Master later introduces a materially new data practice, AFAMEE will review and update the policy and applicable store disclosures as required.
